- **USBridge Remote**: An open‑source peer‑to‑peer remote desktop tool that integrates Moonlight and natively supports Wayland. Achieves low‑latency local remote control without a central server or sign‑up, tailor‑made for self‑hosted remote access demands.

Wrap it as a Chrome extension that lets users export with one click when viewing their purchase history on Amazon, Apple, Google Play, and similar platforms. **Key judgment**: The product is a Chrome extension shaped as a "purchase history exporter + offline ownership safe." Monetization: one‑time $9 for unlimited exports; the free tier limits you to 10 records. The technical core: using Gemini Flash API's multimodal ability to extract structured data from screenshots. Weekend extension paths: integrate IMAP to parse purchase confirmation emails directly from Gmail, automatically monitor changes to platform refund policies, and proactively alert users to export when a platform begins removing purchased content.
