## Which tool changes are blocking developers?

- **Backup system co-reachable with the primary system**: Attackers wipe both primary and backup simultaneously. Developers must ensure backup unreachable, verify offline copies, or isolate audit trails — otherwise bear full responsibility for total data loss.
- **Gemini Flash model series deprecation and forced upgrade with price hike**: Google abruptly deprecates old Flash models and launches a more expensive replacement. Price-sensitive workloads are forced to migrate with zero notice and swallow higher operating costs.
